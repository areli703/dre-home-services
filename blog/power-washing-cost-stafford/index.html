Power Washing Cost in Stafford, VA: 2026 Pricing Guide

Published 2026-06-14 by Dre Home Services LLC

If you're a homeowner in Stafford, Fredericksburg, or Woodbridge wondering what professional power washing costs in 2026, you're in the right place. Whether it's a grimy driveway, a mildew-covered siding, or a deck that's turned gray, power washing restores your home's exterior — and the price varies more than most people expect.

Average Power Washing Costs in Central Virginia (2026)

Based on our 500+ completed projects across Stafford, Fredericksburg, Woodbridge, King George, Caroline, and Spotsylvania, here's what homeowners are paying in 2026:

  • House Washing (single-story): $250 – $450
  • House Washing (two-story): $400 – $800
  • Driveway Cleaning: $150 – $350
  • Deck Washing & Brightening: $200 – $500
  • Fence Washing: $150 – $400
  • Roof Soft Wash: $350 – $700
  • Full Exterior Package (house + driveway + deck): $600 – $1,200

What Affects Your Power Washing Cost?

1. Surface Type & Square Footage

A 1,200 sq. ft. rancher in Stafford costs less than a 3,000 sq. ft. colonial in Fredericksburg with multiple gables and dormers. Vinyl siding cleans faster than brick or stucco, which require lower pressure and more care. Driveways range from compact single-car pads to sprawling circular drives — square footage is the single biggest cost driver.

2. Level of Grime, Mold & Mildew

Virginia's humid summers create ideal conditions for mildew, algae, and the stubborn black streaks you see on north-facing siding. A lightly dusty house might need a straightforward wash, but a home with years of organic buildup requires pretreatment with sodium hypochlorite-based cleaners, adding $50–$150 to the job. In Woodbridge and King George, homes near wooded lots often accumulate more pollen and sap residue, increasing cleaning time.

3. Accessibility & Height

Two-story homes and houses on steep lots require extension wands, ladder work, or lift equipment. A walkout basement with exposed foundation walls adds surface area. Homes in Caroline County with wide setbacks and limited hose access may need additional equipment staging, which can add $75–$150.

4. Soft Wash vs. Pressure Wash

Not every surface can handle high pressure. Roof shingles, aging wood decks, and older vinyl siding all benefit from soft washing — a low-pressure method using specialized cleaning solutions that kill mold and algae at the root. Soft washing typically costs 15–25% more than standard pressure washing but protects your surfaces from damage. At Dre Home Services, we assess every surface and choose the right method — we never blast a roof with high pressure just to save time.

Power Washing vs. DIY: Why Professional Matters

Renting a pressure washer from a home improvement store runs $50–$100 per day, which sounds appealing. But we've repaired damage from DIY pressure washing on dozens of homes across Spotsylvania and Prince William County. Common DIY mistakes include:

  • Etched concrete: Holding the nozzle too close leaves permanent swirl marks on driveways
  • Water intrusion: Spraying upward under siding forces water behind the vapor barrier, leading to mold inside walls
  • Gouged wood: High pressure on decks splinters soft grain and raises fibers, requiring sanding to fix
  • Stripped paint: Too much pressure on painted surfaces means an unplanned repaint job
  • Falling from ladders: Two-story washing from a ladder with a kickback wand is a leading cause of homeowner injuries

Professional power washing costs more upfront but eliminates these risks. Our equipment is commercial-grade with adjustable pressure, heated water options for tough grease, and the right cleaning agents for each surface.

Seasonal Timing for Virginia Homeowners

In Central Virginia, the best time for power washing is April through October. Winter washing is possible but slower — cold water is less effective at breaking down grime, and surfaces take longer to dry. Spring is the most popular season: homeowners want to clean off winter salt residue, pollen, and mold before outdoor entertaining begins. Fall is ideal for pre-winter prep — clearing gutters, washing siding before freeze-thaw cycles, and cleaning decks before they're covered in leaves.

We recommend booking 2–3 weeks ahead for spring and early summer slots, especially in Stafford and Fredericksburg where demand peaks in May and June.
