How to Choose a Licensed Contractor in Central Virginia

Published 2026-04-20 by Dre Home Services LLC

Hiring the wrong contractor can turn your dream renovation into a financial nightmare. In Virginia, the home improvement industry sees hundreds of complaints annually — from abandoned projects to shoddy work to outright fraud. If you're in Fredericksburg, Stafford, Woodbridge, or anywhere in Central Virginia, here's exactly how to protect yourself.

Step 1: Verify the License

Virginia requires a Class A, B, or C contractor license for any project over $1,000. Here's how to check:

  • Visit DPOR License Lookup
  • Enter the contractor's business name or license number
  • Verify the license is active (not expired or revoked)
  • Confirm the license covers the work you need

Step 2: Confirm Insurance

Every legitimate contractor carries two types of insurance:

  • General Liability: Covers property damage during the project (minimum $500,000 recommended)
  • Workers' Compensation: Covers injuries to workers on your property

Ask for a certificate of insurance (COI) and call the insurance company to verify it's current. At Dre Home Services, we email COIs to every client before work begins.

Step 3: Check Reviews & References

Look beyond star ratings. Read actual reviews on Google, Yelp, and Facebook. Pay attention to:

  • How the contractor responds to negative reviews
  • Mentions of punctuality, cleanup, and communication
  • Whether reviewers mention the same crew members (consistency = real employees)

Step 4: Get Multiple Written Estimates

Get at least 3 detailed, written estimates. Each should include:

  • Specific materials (brand, grade, warranty)
  • Scope of work (what's included and what's not)
  • Timeline with start and completion dates
  • Payment schedule (never pay more than 1/3 upfront)
  • Warranty terms in writing

Step 5: Demand a Written Contract

A proper contract includes:

  • Complete scope of work
  • Materials list with brands and quantities
  • Total price and payment schedule
  • Start and completion dates
  • Permit responsibilities
  • Warranty details (workmanship and materials)
