Waterproofing

Waterproofing & Traffic Coating

Water damage is one of the most expensive problems a homeowner can face. Our waterproofing and traffic coating services protect flat roofs, balconies, parking areas, and foundations from moisture intrusion.

We use industry-grade membranes and coatings designed for Virginia's weather — hot summers, freezing winters, and everything in between.
