Your Privacy Rights

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Exercise your right to opt out under the CCPA and Virginia VCDPA.

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Our Commitment

Dre Home Services LLC does not sell your personal information for money. However, certain data sharing practices — such as the use of Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to measure site performance — may be considered "sharing" or use of "sensitive personal information" under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA).

How to Opt Out

You may submit a "Do Not Sell or Share" request by any of the following methods:

Option 1: Email

Send a request to info@drehomeservicesllc.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share Request" and include your full name and the email address or phone number associated with your account.

Option 2: Phone

Call us at (804) 848-9575 and ask to be added to our opt-out list.

Option 3: Use the cookie banner

When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner appears. Click "Decline" to disable non-essential cookies and analytics tracking for your browser.

What Happens After You Opt Out

  • We will stop sharing your personal information with third-party analytics providers within 15 business days.
  • We will retain a record of your opt-out request as required by law, but will not use your information for marketing or analytics.
  • Your opt-out preference will be stored in your browser (via the cookie banner) for 365 days. After that, you will be prompted to confirm your choice again.
  • You may still receive essential service communications related to active quotes, scheduled appointments, or service contracts — these are not "sharing" under the law.

Authorized Agents

If you are submitting a request on behalf of another person, you must provide written authorization signed by the consumer along with verification of your own identity. Email info@drehomeservicesllc.com for the authorization form.

Non-Discrimination

Dre Home Services LLC will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights. You will receive the same services, pricing, and quality whether or not you opt out.

More Information

For full details on what we collect and how we use it, see our Privacy Policy. For general terms, see our Terms of Service.
